Mathematical & Musicological Engraving Foundations

ScoreLint utilizes exact rational arithmetic and acoustic frequency calculations to eliminate timing drift and engraving ambiguities:

1. Exact Fractional Measure Beat Budget: $$\sum_{k=1}^{N} \frac{\text{duration}_k}{4 \cdot \text{divisions}} = \frac{\text{beats}}{\text{beat-type}}$$ Where each voice must account for precisely the declared whole note fraction (e.g. $4/4 = 1$, $3/4 = 3/4$, $6/8 = 3/4$). Floats are forbidden to prevent rhythmic drift across long scores.
2. Acoustic Pitch Frequency & Equal Temperament: $$f(d) = 440 \times 2^{\frac{d - 69}{12}} \text{ Hz}$$ Where $d$ is the MIDI note number ($C4 = 60$, $A4 = 69$). Enharmonic ties ($F\sharp \leftrightarrow G\flat$) must preserve written pitch identity on tied noteheads.
3. Tuplet Rational Time Modification: $$\Delta t_{\text{tuplet}} = \Delta t_{\text{nominal}} \times \left(\frac{\text{normal-notes}}{\text{actual-notes}}\right)$$ For example, a standard triplet modifies duration by $2/3$, requiring exact numerator/denominator conservation to maintain barlines.
